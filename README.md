As WorkOS is hosted and operated by third-party providers, in line with our Information Security compliance requirements, we cannot integrate it with our infrastructure without a legal/business agreement and a Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA) with the provider.

Additionally, we need to perform a security risk assessment of the platform before onboarding access to our infrastructure for any third-party provider.

Please confirm whether your organization has an existing agreement and NDA with WorkOS, and share any reports if you have already conducted a risk assessment of WorkOS covering the below criteria. So that, we will get this validated internally and will plan to initiate the next steps on this requirement.
1.	Data elements read/ access by "workos".
2.	Data encryption standards (both Data-in-transit & Data-at-rest).
3.	Backup and BCP plan on the platform.
4.	Security measures & DLP implemented on live & Backup data.
5.	VAPT Assessment reports on the application.
6.	ISO/ SOC2 reports provided by "Workos".
7.	Data retention and secure data disposal practices.
8.	Data separation from other customers & infrastructure.
9.	Patch Management policy & practices implemented on the product.
10.	Vulnerability management policy & practices implemented on the product.
11.	Encryption policy & practices implemented on the product.
